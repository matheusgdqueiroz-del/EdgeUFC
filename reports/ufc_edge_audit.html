The short answer

A small, repeatable edge — biggest when lines first open

On the locked holdout (Jan 2025 to Sep 2026, 878 fights) the engine’s probabilities beat the closing line by +0.0064 log-loss per fight and the opening line by +0.0153. That is modest in accuracy terms — both sides pick about the same winners — but it is where money is made: the engine is better at saying how likely, not just who.

Would it have made money?

Cumulative units, one disciplined rule the whole way

Rule: bet only when expected value exceeds 3%, stake 1/8 Kelly, never more than 1.5 units (1 unit = 1% of bankroll), one bet per fight, draws lose, no-contests refund. Every probability behind every bet came from a model trained only on earlier fights.

Which information carries signal?

Records from outside the UFC matter more than cage statistics

Market-free model, development years, log loss as each source is added (longer bar = better). Grey bars are the betting lines for scale.

UFCStats striking and grappling numbers alone reach 0.652. The biggest single jumps came from each fighter’s full professional record on Sherdog (pre-UFC fights, a global Elo across every promotion) and from FightMatrix’s pre-fight ratings. A Bayesian 14-skill model of opponent-adjusted offence and defence was elegant but added nothing the engineered statistics didn’t already carry. Age was the most important single physical variable; reach, height and stance barely moved the needle.

After controlling for the closing line, the corrections that replicated were consistent with an intuitive story: the market underweights defence (strikes absorbed, striking defence) and wrestling pressure (takedown attempts), and slightly overreacts to late line movement.

How well calibrated is it?

When it says 70%, it wins about 70%

EngineClosing line

2021–Sep 2026. Dot size = fights in the bin; dashed line = perfect calibration.

Both sides sit close to the diagonal. The one persistent market flaw is at the extremes: favourites priced above 80% win more often than their price implies, and longshots under 20% are overbet — backing them lost 44% of stake since 2021. The engine inherits the line’s strengths and leans into that tilt, which is where a good share of its gain comes from.

Using it

Double-click, update, compare with Pinnacle

  • Open UFC Edge and press Update & predict. Each fight shows the engine’s probability, the market’s, and a stake at the estimated Pinnacle price.
  • Type the odds Pinnacle actually shows under each fighter: the stake recalculates, and fights without a bet show the price that would make them one.
  • Update as soon as a card’s lines open; that is where the tested edge was largest. Never take a worse price than the minimum shown.
  • Keep the stake rule. Doubling units on a “sure thing” is how a real edge still goes broke.
Method and safeguards

How we tried not to fool ourselves

  • No hindsight by construction. Features are built date by date; each fighter is snapshotted before a fight and updated after. Canary tests delete everything after mid-2016 (and shuffle inputs): all earlier features stay bit-identical.
  • Pre-registration. Design, thresholds and success criteria were written down before scoring 2021–2024, and again before the 2025–2026 holdout. The one failed criterion (late-bet profit in validation) is reported as a failure.
  • Only future-obtainable data. UFCStats (daily mirror), Sherdog, BestFightOdds, FightMatrix, Wikipedia and Open-Meteo can all be re-collected for next week’s card. Wikimedia pageviews were dropped when the API throttled collection.
  • Independent prices. Our scraped odds matched two separate archives (average gap under 2.5 points, including Pinnacle). Development-period profits were re-settled at those archives’ prices and survived.
  • Controls. A recalibrated market alone, and the market plus a random “model”, both fail to make money under the same pipeline.

Known limits: opening-line limits cap real stakes; the UFCStats mirror lags a day; a handful of overturned results are treated as final; FightMatrix can backfill forgotten regional bouts into old ratings.
